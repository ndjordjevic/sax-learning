# Hal Leonard Jazz Play-Along: index of the 130 owned volumes

Built 2026-11-24 from the MP3 track names in pCloud `2 Jazz Methods & Etudes/Hal Leonard Jazz Play-Along Series - Various (Hal Leonard)/HLJazzPA <n> <title>/`. Each volume folder has a score PDF (C, B♭, E♭ and bass editions; page numbers on its contents page) and two MP3s per song: **melody + rhythm section** and **rhythm section only** (the file ending `- playalong`). Titles come from the file names, so spelling follows them.

Three volume numbers appear twice (14, 32 and 45): both folders are listed. Vol. 128 (*Vocal Standards*) is not in the collection.
