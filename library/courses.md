# Courses

## 🔍 BetterSax Foundations (Jay Metcalf): owned video course
The most complete owned resource: a staged curriculum that runs through Phases 1–5 of the [study plan](../plan/study-plan.md). The modules are listed in learning order. Every module covers both horns.

| Module | Level | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **Core Essentials** ✅ | Beginner | Sound, technique, warm-ups. |
| **Pentatonic Foundation** ⏸ skipped | Beginner | One pentatonic scale in one key. Birch covers 8 of its 10 concepts in more depth; the only real loss is its by-ear work on familiar tunes (O'Neill Ch.11 covers that). |
| **Blues Foundation** | Beginner–Int | Swing/shuffle notation, the 12-bar form, blue notes, the blues scale in several keys (incl. F♯), 3 blues heads, **"Advanced Ear Training Blues"**, the **"Transcribe This?!"** tenor solo. By Phase 3 it's mostly a review of Birch + EE, apart from the last parts. |
| **Blues Language** | Beginner | 2–5-note blues licks through all 12 keys around the cycle of 4ths, learned **by ear** (Metcalf: don't read them). |
| **Harmonic Foundation** | Int (theory: beginner) | **Theory videos 01–05** (Nuts & Bolts, intervals, major scale, chords, functions) are beginner material. Then modes, minor scales, and the circle of 4ths/5ths: 12-key drills including altered and diminished chords ("years of practice"). |
| **Magic of Melody** | Int → Adv | Parts I–II: melody-first soloing over *Just Friends* + a blues. Part III etudes: *All of Me* ♩=145, *Autumn Leaves* 170, *Confirmation* and *Donna Lee* at 200. |
| 🔍 **Chord Tone Zone** (taught on tenor by Adam Larson) | Advanced beginner (drills) → Advanced (etudes) | Deep-checked 2026-11-21. **Drills** (90 short videos, about 1 min each) on a 12-bar blues (concert B♭) and *There Will Never Be Another You* (concert E♭). Each tune has 5 one- or two-bar rhythm cells (mostly quarters and 8ths). Every cell is played on the root, 3rd, 5th and 7th, then the pairs R+3, R+5, R+7, 3+5 and **3+7 (guide tones)**: one chord tone per chord through the whole form. Larson plays it, then a "Now you try!" screen. That's the same work as step 5 of [how to learn a standard](../band/how-to-learn-a-standard.md), on other tunes. **Etudes:** demo videos plus recordings (about 50–75 s) on *TWNBAY, Just Friends, Lady Bird, ATTYA, Stella*. **Bonus:** a 51-min masterclass on digital patterns (1212, 2121, 1231, 3213, 12345321…) and two PDFs: *Digital Patterns/Variations* on a ii–V–I, and *Digital patterns over a B♭ blues* + a 2-5-1 lick (concert B♭; only pp.2–3, p.1 is missing). 6 backing tracks, one tempo each. ⚠️ **The main course PDF is not owned** and membership access has lapsed. The drills still work without it (tenor: blues in C, *TWNBAY* in F); the etudes can only be learned by ear. |
| **Melodic Etudes / Essential Jazz Repertoire Vol.2** | Int → Adv | Two **Shuffle Blues** etudes (Bb + F, ♩=120), then written solos over standards: *Fly Me to the Moon* ♩=136 (D major / B minor), *Rhythm Changes* ♩=166, *Take Five* ♩=170 in 5/4. No key signatures in any etude. |

## 🔍 BetterSax Shed: free bonus PDFs with the membership
The PDFs and backing tracks for BetterSax's YouTube lessons: 35 files (jazz, theory, improv, technique) downloaded 2026-11-13 to pCloud `4 Video Courses/BetterSax Shed - Jay Metcalf (BetterSax)/`, in 8 section folders.

Most useful for the standards goal:
- 🔍 **Harmonic Building Blocks (3 Jazz Standards)** (4 pp, concert key). One page lists 11 chord patterns that make up most standards:
  - the major ii–V–I and its variants (to the IV chord, backdoor, ii–V up a minor 3rd);
  - the same chord quality moving up a 4th;
  - the minor ii–V–i;
  - the side door;
  - the I–vi–ii–V turnaround;
  - the dominant II chord;
  - starting on the IV chord.

  Then come lead sheets of *Autumn Leaves*, *There Will Never Be Another You* and *Just Friends* with every pattern labelled. On tenor, read the chords a whole step up.
- **All of Me Improvisation Patterns** and **Jazz 101: Approach Tones and Enclosures** (6 pp each; alto, tenor and concert key).
- **Easy ii–V–I Lick**, **3 Beginner Jazz Exercises**, and **5 Levels of Improvisation Over Minor Chords** (concert key).
- **Scales You Need for Jazz** (12 scales in all keys), **Major Scales in All 12 Keys**, **Music Theory Cheat Sheet**, **Transposition Guide**.
- **Play Sax by Ear Cheatsheet** (23 pp), pentatonic patterns and licks with backing tracks, and a blues improv lesson.
- Later: altissimo charts, Chad LB's chromatic approach notes, and solo transcriptions (*Mo' Better Blues*, Baptiste Herbin on *There Is No Greater Love*).
