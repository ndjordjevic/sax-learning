## 🗓️ Daily plan (Phase 0)

1. **Long tones.**
2. **Essential Elements and/or Saxophon spielen Band 2.** Band 2: the next song (now #6 *Guantanamera*). EE: in order from Ex.1, stop at Ex.39.
3. **Reading Jazz:** the next duet (now Duet 5), as written.
4. **Band standard melody (main focus):** play the melody from the 557 over a backing track (iReal Pro, Quartet…). Do one band tune after another until every band tune's melody is learned. Listen to a couple of versions first. Now: [*Autumn Leaves*](tunes/autumn-leaves.md) → next [*All Blues*](tunes/all-blues.md).
5. **Start improvising, slowly.** Begin on *Autumn Leaves* and add more tunes over time. The end goal is to open any standard, read the chords and improvise.
   - Work on guide tones, chord tones and scale tones, using the techniques in the Playbook, Let's Play Jazz! and Birch *Rhythm Changes Soloing* (Ch.1–4, chord-tone soloing, first), plus chord tones over a drone.
   - **Analyze each tune:** its form (AABA, ABAC, blues…) and its progressions (ii–V–Is, turnarounds).

**Off the sax:** ear training (Aebersold *Jazz Ear Training*) · jazz theory (BetterSax Harmonic Foundation videos 01–05) · listening.

**Weekly:** the band, reading from the 557. Until the Bb edition arrives, read tenor a whole step up.

**Notes**
- Reading Jazz: play it as written for now. Add an improvise day once step 4 has given you something to try.
- Birch *Beginner Jazz Soloing* (pentatonics) is on hold (since 2026-11-21), so the melody and chord/guide tones come first.
- Workload: 2–3 books a day at most.
