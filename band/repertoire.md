# Band repertoire (57 tunes)

The weekly band plays from the **557 Jazz Standards** (C edition, concert pitch; a Bb edition is on order). Until it arrives, read tenor a whole step up, or use the tenor lead sheets transposed from the 557 in pCloud `6 Fake Books/Band Tunes/<Tune>/`, named `<Tune> - Bb Tenor Sax (from 557 Jazz Standards).pdf`. So far there's one: *Autumn Leaves*, checked note for note against 557 p.21.

Tune list from a bandmate's recordings list (2026-11-18). The 557 page numbers are checked on the scans. ♩ = the bandmate's tempo; their notes are translated. Coverage was checked 2026-11-22.

**Columns**

- **Notes:** PB = a *Jazz Standards Playbook* Vol. 1 study · WB = a *Standards Workbook* tune.
- **Status:** 🔄 learning · ⏭ next · later: *can follow* / *solid*. Update it as tunes are learned.
- **Other fake books** (owned, `6 Fake Books/`): RB2 = *Real Book Vol. 2* old edition, Bb (book page) · SRB = *Standards Real Book* (C; the most accurate, use it to check doubtful notes) · RBB = *Real Book of Blues* (C, vocal keys: check the key) · AB = *AB Real Book* (Bb). *Real Book Vol. 1* (Bb + C) isn't mapped here yet.
- **Quartet:** the volume the tune was found in; — = not in Quartet.
- **Tomplay** (tenor): Easy = has an Easy/Intermediate level · other = harder levels only. ✅ = bought.
- **Hal Leonard** (not owned, see [wishlist](../library/wishlist.md)): MT = *Real Book Multi-Tracks* vol. · JPA = *Jazz Play-Along* vol. (both play the melody) · EZ = *Easy Jazz Play-Along* vol. (backing only).
- **MuseScore:** community lead sheet (.mxl + PDF) in `6 Fake Books/Lead Sheets - MuseScore community (band tunes)/`. ✅ = downloaded · blocked / — = still to do (see `_PROGRESS.md` in that folder) · none = no usable score.

## Apps for backing tracks

- **iReal Pro** (iOS/Android/Mac, about €15–20, one-time; bought): **57/57** tunes are in the free forum playlist **Jazz 1460** (1,510 tunes; *Black Orpheus* is listed as *Manhã de Carnaval*). Import it from the forum *inside the app*. It's a synthetic trio: any key, any tempo, any number of choruses, and a Bb chart view, so you can always match the 557's key and start slow. **First choice.** It loops the chords only: no melody and no intros.
- **Quartet** (real-musician recordings, about $15 per volume, no subscription; **iPhone/iPad or M-series Mac only**): change key and tempo, loop sections, set repeats, mute any instrument. Repeated choruses are copies of the same take, and very slow tempos are time-stretched. **53/57.** For the 4 missing tunes use "Blues in F" (*Now's the Time*) or "Blues (in all keys)" in B♭ (*Sonnymoon for Two*). Full list: the "A-Z Song List" PDF on quartetapp.com. Second app, for when a tune is comfortable.
- **Tomplay** (a real band with separate bass, drums, piano and **tenor sax** tracks: mute the sax to play the part yourself; tempo and loop; scrolling score). €5.99 per score, or about 30% off in the "Real Book Jazz by Tomplay, Tenor Saxophone" collections. A full performance is about 5 minutes: intro, head ×2, 3 written solo choruses, head ×2, ending. **27/57.** ⚠️ Matches are by title: *All of Me* on Tomplay is only John Legend's pop song, not the jazz standard (the jazz Marks & Simons scores in their old blog post are gone from the catalog, checked 2026-11-23); check the composer before buying. ⚠️ The easier levels can be in a different key from the band (the Very Easy and Easy levels of *All Blues* are in tenor G, not A): check the key before buying.
- Owned audio: the Playbook and *Let's Play Jazz!* backing tracks (*Autumn Leaves* etc.), Aebersold.

## Where to hear the melody

- **Recordings:** the "Getting Started" picks on jazzstandards.com; a YouTube band playlist. Method and apps: `reports/Jazz standard melody learning resources.md`.
- No app is "iReal Pro with melody". Hear the head from a recording, then read it from the 557 over iReal Pro. The Hal Leonard MT and JPA books (column above) are the only ones that play the melody.
