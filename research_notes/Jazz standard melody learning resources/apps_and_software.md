# Apps and software for learning the melody (head) of jazz standards

Scope: tools that help an advanced-beginner tenor (Bb) player learn the head of a band standard, by ear from recordings and from charts. Research date: 2026-11-22. Prices are US unless noted, and many came from app-store snapshots or third-party trackers, so check them at checkout.

## 1. Slow-down / loop / pitch-shift tools for learning heads from recordings

### Takeaway
Two tools come up most often for learning tunes by ear. Anytune (iOS/Mac) gets the most sax-teacher endorsements, BetterSax's among them. Transcribe! is the standard desktop tool and the only paid one with a native Linux build. For free use, browser extensions such as "Transpose" handle YouTube slow-down, A-B loops and semitone shifts. Every tool here can pitch-shift, but none sells a dedicated "Bb mode". To hear a concert recording at tenor written pitch you would shift it by a whole step, which is rarely useful: the usual practice is to leave the recording in concert pitch and learn to finger along.

### Cited Findings
**Transcribe! (Seventh String): desktop, Linux included**
- Runs on Windows, Mac and Linux, but not on phones or tablets. The Linux build is 64-bit only. — [Seventh String buy page](https://seventhstring.com/xscribe/buy.html)
- The price is about 37 euros. The exact amount appears in the FastSpring checkout and is localized by country. Older third-party listings give $39. — [Seventh String buy page](https://seventhstring.com/xscribe/buy.html); [KVR Audio](https://www.kvraudio.com/product/transcribe-by-seventhstring)
- The latest release is v9.60 (4 July 2026). The Linux download page was still offering 9.51.1, so the Linux build may lag behind. — [Release history](https://seventhstring.com/xscribe/history.html); [Linux download](https://seventhstring.com/xscribe/download_linux.html)
- Also on Flathub (com.seventhstring.Transcribe) and the AUR. — [Flathub](https://flathub.org/hi/apps/com.seventhstring.Transcribe); [AUR](https://aur.archlinux.org/packages/transcribe)
- One saxophonist on a sax forum reports using Transcribe! and Amazing Slow Downer and calls both "great". Another forum user pairs Transcribe! with pencil and staff paper. — [Cafe Saxophone thread](https://cafesaxophone.com/threads/what-how-and-why-are-you-transcribing.13137/page-4)

**Anytune (iOS/Mac)**
- BetterSax (Jay Metcalf) recommends Anytune Pro for transcribing by ear. It loops sections, slows playback and changes key, and Jay uses it to learn one phrase at a time and then loop the phrases together. — [BetterSax, "7 Amazing Apps for Saxophone Players"](https://bettersax.com/7-amazing-apps-for-saxophone-players-you-probably-didnt-know-existed/)
- A sax blog calls it "best for learning songs by ear and transcription". Reviewers note that it changes pitch and tempo independently. — [Jazzfuel, Six Saxophone Apps](https://jazzfuel.com/saxophone-apps/); [Preply](https://preply.com/en/blog/best-saxophone-learning-apps/)
- "Anytune Pro+: Studio Tools" is $14.99 on the App Store with no in-app purchases (snapshot from March 2026). — [App Pricing Lab](https://www.apppricinglab.com/app/apple/478293637)

**Capo (SuperMegaUltraGroovy, Mac/iOS)**
- Still maintained. The Mac App Store shows v4.6 dated 10 Oct 2025. In-app tiers include Capo Pro yearly at $39.99 and Capo Platinum at $49.99. — [Mac App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/capo/id696977615?mt=12&ls=1)
- A Norwegian App Store page says "contact us … to learn more about how this change affects you", which suggests a business-model change (probably to a subscription). The details are unknown. — [App Store NO](https://apps.apple.com/no/app/capo/id696977615?mt=12)
- An unrelated Firefox extension, also named "Capo – Pitch & Speed Changer" (Elyra Labs), offers pitch, key and speed changes and a loop-by-loop speed trainer. It is "free forever", with a paid subscription for extras. — [Firefox Add-ons](https://addons.mozilla.org/en-US/firefox/addon/capo-pitch-speed-changer/)

**Other mobile slow-downers**
- AudioScrub (REMIX Edition) for iOS slows playback to 25% with pitch held, for $3.99. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id903693369)
- "Blue Note – Learn Music by Ear" (iOS) is built for learning solos by ear. It slows playback without changing pitch and has a video zoom for fingerings. — [AppAdvice](https://appadvice.com/app/bluenote-learn-music-by-ear/1587389494)

**Browser extensions for YouTube (free, work on Linux and Mac)**
- "Transpose ▲▼ pitch ▹ speed ▹ loop for videos" (Chrome and Edge) shifts pitch by semitones in real time, changes speed, and sets unlimited loops between time markers. It works on YouTube and Vimeo, though some sites block it. About 900k Chrome users. — [Chrome Web Store](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/transpose-pitch-loop-for/ioimlbgefgadofblnajllknopjboejda); [Edge Add-ons](https://microsoftedge.microsoft.com/addons/detail/transpose-%E2%96%B2%E2%96%BC-pitch-%E2%96%B9-spee/nakcigkhphkecnebinpgpdpbjfjgilkl)
- Firefox alternatives:
  - Etude · Looper: any key, fine-tuning in cents, and saved named A-B loops for each video. — [Firefox](https://addons.mozilla.org/firefox/addon/etude-looper/)
  - MusicTools for YouTube: 0.25x–4x speed with pitch locked, and ±12 semitones. — [Firefox](https://addons.mozilla.org/addon/musictools-for-youtube/)
  - Looposer: loop timestamps, transposition, and fine-tuning of A from 400 to 480 Hz. — [Firefox](https://addons.mozilla.org/firefox/addon/looposer/)
- JamKit (Chrome): ±12 semitone shift without changing speed, plus A/B loops. — [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/jamkit-musicians-toolbox/nhdomeochikggchnejdipcaiigcbldcp)

**AI stem separation (Moises)**
- Moises' stem-separation page lists 27 stem types. Among them are separate "Wind" and "Woodwind" stems, plus piano, keys, strings and guitar. — [Moises stem separation](https://moises.ai/features/stem-separation/); [Moises help center](https://help.moises.ai/hc/en-us/articles/19247459645724-How-do-I-separate-my-tracks-using-the-Custom-Upload-on-the-Mobile-App)
- Available on iOS, Android and web. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/moises-the-musicians-app/id1515796612); [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=ai.moises&hl=en_US)
- Pricing: the US App Store lists Premium at $5.99/month or $39.99/year and Pro at $29.99/month (Oct 2026, matching a March 2026 snapshot). Third-party reviews give different numbers (Premium $3.99/month, Pro $9.99/month), so treat those with caution. A free tier allows a limited number of uploads per month. — [App Pricing Lab](https://apppricinglab.com/iap/apple/1515796612); [StemSplit review](https://stemsplit.io/blog/moises-ai-review); [G2](https://www.g2.com/products/moises-ai/pricing)
- Moises also has tempo and pitch control and chord detection. — [StemSplit review](https://stemsplit.io/blog/moises-ai-review)
- Wikipedia notes that by 2025, separation of piano, strings, winds and guitar was offered by many developers. — [Wikipedia, Music source separation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Music_source_separation)

### Inferences
- Linux desktop: Transcribe! (paid, native) or a browser extension on YouTube (free) covers everything needed to slow, loop and pitch-shift a head. Mac or phone: Anytune is the sax-community favourite. Most of these tools overlap, so pick one rather than several.
- For learning a head, the most useful features are an A-B loop on one phrase, 60–75% speed, and in Transcribe!/Anytune saved markers per phrase or section. Pitch-shifting to tenor key is not needed for playing along: a concert recording sounds correct while you play written-Bb fingerings. A shift helps only to match a recording that is slightly off A=440 (the fine-tune cents control).
- Moises's "Wind" stem could isolate or remove the sax/trumpet melody of a recording, so you can hear the head alone or play it over the rhythm section. The project's own audio doc (`audio-processing-methodology.md`) found that Demucs' vocal stem misses sax, so quality for horns should be tested per track, not assumed.
- Audacity and VLC (free, Linux) can also slow audio down, but I did not research them here. They are general-purpose, with clunkier looping than the dedicated tools.

### Gaps
- No current price found for Amazing Slow Downer (Roni Music). No source confirmed whether "Song Master" is still sold.
- No independent test found of how well Moises's Wind stem isolates a tenor sax or trumpet head on a jazz combo recording.
- Reddit threads did not appear in search results, so there is no direct Reddit consensus here.
- No source found for which of these tools named teachers (other than BetterSax) recommend specifically for heads, as distinct from solos.

## 2. Chart / backing apps: which ones include the melody?

### Takeaway
iReal Pro has no melody. It is a chords-and-form backing band with Bb transposition. Tools that do include or play the melody are:
- Hal Leonard's Real Book Multi-Tracks: sax or trumpet plays the head, with Bb charts and mutable parts.
- Tomplay: tenor-sax jazz collections with a sax part you can mute and a scrolling score.
- Band-in-a-Box: MIDI melodist/soloist features.
- Calypso Score's New Bob Book and iGigBook: transposable lead sheets.

PDF readers such as forScore and MobileSheets cannot transpose a scanned fake book.

### Cited Findings
**iReal Pro**
- No melody. The App Store describes it as a backing band plus chord charts. Forum replies say "there is no facility inside iReal Pro to add melody (notation)", and users note that melodies are left out (one thread suggests copyright reasons). — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id298206806); [iReal forums](https://forums.irealpro.com/goto/post?id=72534); [iReal forums, copyright thread](https://www.irealb.com/forums/showthread.php?p=49278)
- LearnJazzStandards: "While the iReal Pro app gives you the common chord progressions for songs, it doesn't give you the melody." It also calls the MIDI swing "mechanical". — [LearnJazzStandards, "5 Ways iReal Pro Will Help You…"](https://www.learnjazzstandards.com/?p=184687)
- Bb transposition: a global Eb/Bb/F/G transposition setting for horn players, and any chart can be moved to any key. Recent reviews complain that a 2026 update moved the transposing-instrument control. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/298206806)
- Price: about $19.99 on iOS (€22.99 or CHF 20 in other storefronts), with extra styles sold in-app. — [App Store GB/CH listings](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/id298206806)
- Workarounds: export iReal chords as MIDI and add the melody in MuseScore, or attach iReal audio to a PDF in MobileSheets. — [MuseScore forum](https://musescore.org/en/node/299582); [iReal forums](https://forums.irealpro.com/goto/post?id=70485)

**Hal Leonard Real Book Multi-Tracks Play-Along (book + online audio)**
- 10 songs per volume. Lead sheets come in C, Bb, Eb and bass clef. On the audio, sax or trumpet plays the melody for the head in and head out. Features: tempo control, looping, on/off buttons for each instrument, and a follow-along marker on the lead sheet. Full stereo tracks can be downloaded. Won SBO's 2019 Best Jazz Teaching Tool award. — [Hal Leonard series page](https://www.halleonard.com/series/RBPLYA)
- 15 volumes at $17.99–$19.99 each. Titles include Maiden Voyage, Miles Davis, All Blues, Charlie Parker, Sonny Rollins, Monk, Bebop Era, Coltrane, 1950s/60s/70s Jazz, and Christmas volumes. — [Hal Leonard series page](https://www.halleonard.com/series/RBPLYA)

**Sheet Music Direct (Hal Leonard app)**
- Over 25,000 lead sheets, including official Real Book charts, with audio playback and tempo control. The listing does not say whether the playback includes the melody. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id499069309)

**Tomplay (iOS/Android/desktop)**
- Tenor-sax jazz series, all "saxophone with accompaniment": *Real Book Jazz by Tomplay* (Beginner through Advanced, several volumes) and *The Best of Jazz for Tenor Saxophone*. Examples include Satin Doll, Misty, Blue Train and Autumn Leaves. — [Tomplay Real Book Jazz Tenor Adv. Vol. 1](https://tomplay.com/saxophone-sheet-music/real-book-jazz-by-tomplay-tenor-saxophone-advanced-vol-1-saxophone-tenor-saxophone-score); [Best of Jazz Tenor Int-Adv Vol. 2](https://tomplay.com/index.php/saxophone-sheet-music/the-best-of-jazz-for-tenor-saxophone-intermediate-advanced-vol-2-saxophone-score)
- BetterSax lists Tomplay's features: scrolling sheet music, a sax part you can mute, chord changes, real-time fingering charts, record and playback, and adjustable tempo and looping. — [BetterSax](https://bettersax.com/7-amazing-apps-for-saxophone-players-you-probably-didnt-know-existed/)
- There is a 14-day free unlimited-access trial and discounted bundles (for example "5 Scores 30% OFF"). No list price was visible. — [Tomplay listing](https://tomplay.com/saxophone-sheet-music/real-book-jazz-by-tomplay-tenor-saxophone-intermediate-vol-1-saxophone-tenor-saxophone-score)

**Band-in-a-Box (PG Music; Windows/Mac desktop, no Linux)**
- The 2026 version is out. Retail is about $96 for Pro and about $357–$469 for UltraPAK (third-party listings). — [Equipboard](https://equipboard.com/items/pg-music-band-in-a-box-2026-ultrapak-for-windows); [PG Music PlusPAK](https://www.pgmusic.com/bbmac.packages.pluspak.htm)
- Includes MIDI Soloists (300+) and MIDI Melodists (200+). These counts come from a 2023 PG Music page. — [PG Music LabPAKs](https://www.pgmusic.com/educators/educators.purchase.win.htm)

**Play-along apps built on real musicians**
- Quartet 1 (UK Music Apps, iOS): 150 jazz standards recorded by real players (piano, guitar, sax, trumpet, bass, drums). You can mute your own instrument, change key on the fly, set a global Bb/Eb chart transposition (v2.2), slow down without changing pitch, and loop with a set number of repeats. $27.90 one-time. Quartet 2 and a bundle also exist. The listing does not say whether a horn plays the head. — [App Store Quartet 1](https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/quartet-1/id1623038041); [Quartet bundle](https://apps.apple.com/us/app-bundle/quartet-bundle-volumes-1-2-the-live-jazz-standards/id1698502024)
- Genius Jamtracks (iOS): a trio-style backing generator with a large catalog of standards. BetterSax says it sounds better than iReal Pro. It is a backing generator, not a melody tool. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1131210331); [BetterSax](https://bettersax.com/7-amazing-apps-for-saxophone-players-you-probably-didnt-know-existed/)

**PDF readers and transposition**
- forScore can't detect notes on a PDF page, so it "isn't able to transpose the music or play" it. — [forScore support](https://forscore.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/151000204975-transposing-and-music-playback)
- A MuseScore forum post says neither forScore nor MobileSheets can transpose image-based PDFs. The post is undated. — [MuseScore forum](https://musescore.org/en/node/90286)
- Fake-book apps that do transpose:
  - iGigBook switches between the Bb, C, Eb and bass-clef versions of the same tune.
  - Calypso Score offers the free New Bob Book (666 songs), with melody and chords transposable to any key.
  - Realbook Master exports transposed sheet music as PDF.
  — [App Store, iGigBook](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6470054573); [App Store, Calypso Score](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id480475970); [App Store, Realbook Master](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6754005659)
- Newzik's help page describes transposing scores in its iPad app. — [Newzik](https://newzik.com/en/resources-sheet-music-app/how-to-transpose-sheet-music-app)

### Inferences
- The user already has tenor (Bb) lead sheets transposed from the 557 (see the pCloud `6 Fake Books/` folder). A plain PDF reader (forScore on iPad, MobileSheets on Android) is therefore enough for reading at band. Transposition matters only for tunes not yet converted, and MuseScore (free, Linux) can produce those PDFs.
- To hear the written melody played back, the cheap options are:
  1. Enter the head into MuseScore and play it back (free, Linux), optionally over iReal-exported chords.
  2. Real Book Multi-Tracks volumes, where available.
  3. Tomplay, for the standards it covers.
- The "listen → learn head" loop works best with a real recording plus a slow-downer (section 1). iReal Pro is then for playing the learned head over the form, as a check that you can keep your place.

### Gaps
- Not researched in this pass (tool-call budget): SmartMusic's jazz content, the MuseScore app's (musescore.com) handling of jazz standards, Chordify's melody features, Open Studio / Jazz Lesson Videos, Songsterr-style sax apps, and LearnJazzStandards' paid products.
- I could not confirm whether Quartet's recordings include the head played by a horn.
- No 2026 official Band-in-a-Box price, and no check of whether its melodist covers specific standards.

## 3. Ear-training apps as they relate to learning heads

### Takeaway
Only lightly covered. Dedicated melody ear-trainers exist (for example MelodEar and "Saxophone by Ear"), but sax sources point to learning heads straight from recordings rather than through drills.

### Cited Findings
- "Saxophone by Ear" (iOS) is free and aimed at beginners, with scales and tunes by ear and a note chart. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6443945225)
- MelodEar is an iOS ear-training app. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1597264296)
- LearnJazzStandards' core advice is to "listen to as many versions of songs as you can find", and it names no specific ear-training app. — [LearnJazzStandards](https://www.learnjazzstandards.com/?p=184687)

## 4. Free vs paid; platforms (summary)

### Takeaway
Free tools on Linux (browser extensions, MuseScore, Audacity, VLC) cover the core needs. The paid tool most worth buying for Linux is Transcribe! (about €37, one-time). On Mac or phone, Anytune (about $15 on iOS) and iReal Pro (about $20) are the standard pair. Moises is subscription-only beyond its free tier.

- Nothing found was discontinued. Capo is still updated, though its pricing model may have changed.
- A minimal stack for this user:
  - Linux: a YouTube extension or Transcribe! for the recording, plus MuseScore for checking written notes.
  - Mac/phone: Anytune or Moises for recordings, iReal Pro for the form.
  - The existing Bb 557 PDFs in forScore or MobileSheets for reading.

### Gaps
