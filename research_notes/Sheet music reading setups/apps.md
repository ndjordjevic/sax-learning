# Sheet-music reader apps (2025–2026): comparison for a tenor player on MobileSheets/iPad

Research date: 2026-11-23. App Store listings fetched that day. Their version dates show month and day but no year, so I assume the 2026 release cycle. Most "comparison" articles online come from **vendors** (Newzik and nkoda blogs). Most user opinion comes from the **MobileSheets forum**, so it is self-selected toward MobileSheets. I found no Reddit threads through search. Each claim below is tagged [vendor], [user], [3rd-party] or [store listing].

## Which apps are most used, platforms and pricing

### Takeaway
forScore is the established iPad leader. It is Apple-only, costs a one-time $24.99, and has an optional $14.99/yr Pro subscription. MobileSheets is the main cross-platform rival: Android, Windows, iOS/iPadOS and macOS, a one-time $15.99 on iOS, no subscription. Newzik is subscription-based and aimed at ensembles. The rest are niche: store-locked readers (Musicnotes, Sheet Music Direct, nkoda, Henle), lyric/chord setlist apps (OnSong, SongBook), or jazz-specific apps (iGigBook, unrealBook, iReal Pro).

### Inferences
- For a solo jazz player reading from PDFs, the real choice is MobileSheets vs forScore. Newzik's main extras (ensemble page-turn control, OMR transposition) matter mostly to orchestras and classical ensembles. Store-locked apps (nkoda, Henle, Musicnotes) add nothing for someone whose library is their own fake-book PDFs.
- MobileSheets is cheaper ($15.99 once vs $24.99 plus optional $14.99/yr) and is the only major app that also runs on Android and Windows.

## Feature comparison for a jazz player reading fake books and lead sheets

### Takeaway
MobileSheets and forScore both cover the core needs: CSV index import to split big PDFs, setlists, Pencil annotation, half-page turns, links and repeats, Bluetooth pedals, face gestures, attached audio and a metronome. MobileSheets has broader formats (ChordPro with chord transposition, ABC, images, text, .docx) and runs on every platform. forScore has the native face-gesture implementation (Pro only), well-documented external-display support, and a far larger iOS user base. Neither can transpose a PDF. Only Newzik offers OMR to convert a PDF to MusicXML and transpose it, and that claim comes from the vendor.

### Cited Findings
- **MobileSheets features** [vendor/store]: it imports CSV index files "to break up large PDF songbooks". It supports ChordPro with chord transposition, face gestures (open mouth, smile) for hands-free turns, and pairing several tablets over WiFi or Bluetooth to show two pages. Version 3.9.44 added .docx files and Nextcloud. Version 3.9.43 fixed ABC rendering and printing. — [App Store MobileSheets](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1620569987?l=en-us)
- MobileSheets also offers [vendor]: annotation layers, stylus, highlights and stamps; horizontal or vertical scrolling; half pages in portrait; two-page view; link points; Bluetooth pedal actions; auto-scroll; an audio player and metronome; library sync over WiFi or cloud; MIDI integration; and import from Dropbox, Google Drive and OneDrive. — [Zubersoft](https://www.zubersoft.com/mobilesheets/)
- MobileSheets transposes ABC files by step count. ChordPro chords transpose with an on-the-fly button. MusicXML is **not** supported as notation: users have asked for it on the forum as a future basis for live transposition [user]. — [Zubersoft forum thread](https://www.zubersoft.com/mobilesheets/forum/showthread.php?tid=2492)
- An iPad MobileSheets user on the forum notes that audio cannot be linked from the iPad Music app [user]. — [Zubersoft forum / appshunter reviews](https://appshunter.io/ios/app/1620569987/reviews/2)
- **forScore features** [vendor]: Apple Pencil annotation without switching modes, setlists, rearranging and bookmarking pages, play-along audio tracks, page-turn devices and MIDI, wireless sync of page turns between nearby devices ("Cue"), iCloud sync, piano keyboard, pitch pipe and practice-time tracking. — [forscore.co](https://forscore.co/); [forScore Cue](https://forscore.co/cue/)
- forScore 15.2.1 added partial page turns in multi-page and landscape Best Fit views, and page turns from an Apple Watch running the Cue app. Version 15.2.6 improved "Reflow" [store listing]. — [App Store forScore](https://apps.apple.com/app/forscore/id363738376)
- **forScore Pro** ($14.99/yr) adds [vendor]: face gestures (head turn, wink, lip movement, depending on device), drag-down pop-up menus, copying the PDF layer with annotations, settings profiles, about 400 extra stamps, time-limited 4SC sharing files, custom metadata icons, Pencil hover gestures and Pro support. — [forScore Pro](https://forscore.co/pro/)
- **forScore external display**: native dual-screen rendering with synced turns and separate display options per screen. It replaced the old "TV Output" in forScore 12. To use AirPlay mirroring instead, you disable forScore's external display first [vendor]. — [forScore support: external display](https://forscore.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/151000204982-displaying-your-music-on-an-external-display); [forScore 12](https://forscore.co/twelve/)
- forScore is described as PDF-only, with no ChordPro [user, MobileSheets forum]. — [Zubersoft forum post](https://zubersoft.com/mobilesheets/forum/post-54560.html)
- A third-party table lists forScore transposition as "No (for reference only)" [competitor blog]. — [Newzik: how to transpose](https://newzik.com/en/resources-sheet-music-app/how-to-transpose-sheet-music-app)
- **Newzik transposition**: MusicXML parts transpose through a "Transposition & Tracks" wheel. A PDF is first converted by OMR ("LiveScore") and can then be transposed [vendor]. Newzik claims about 85–90% accuracy for audio-to-score transcription of a solo melody line in its own 2025 tests [vendor]. — [Newzik support: transposing MusicXML](https://support.newzik.com/en/support/solutions/articles/77000153445-transposing-a-musicxml-part); [Newzik vs MobileSheets](https://newzik.com/en/resources-sheet-music-app/newzik-vs-mobilesheets)
- **Newzik ensemble**: a conductor controls page turns for all musicians, with real-time annotation sharing. Newzik says forScore and MobileSheets lack this [vendor]. Newzik credits MobileSheets with stronger library management, setlists, formats and pedal configuration [vendor admitting a competitor's strength]. — [Newzik vs MobileSheets](https://newzik.com/en/resources-sheet-music-app/newzik-vs-mobilesheets)
- **iGigBook** ships built-in fake-book indexes that one long-time user called "unmatched", plus transposable chord charts. Downsides: no sync or easy backup (one user lost work), clumsy updating of custom PDFs, no macOS version [user, 2023]. — [TidBITS thread](https://talk.tidbits.com/t/choosing-a-sheet-music-manager-for-ipad/21920)
- **iReal Pro** is a strong rehearsal tool but shows chords only, with no melody or PDFs, so it is not a sheet-music manager [user]. — [TidBITS thread](https://talk.tidbits.com/t/choosing-a-sheet-music-manager-for-ipad/21920)

### Inferences
- For transposition, the realistic route for a tenor player is to get Bb lead sheets as PDFs, or to use MuseScore or iReal Pro alongside the reader. MobileSheets already reads ChordPro and ABC, which forScore doesn't, but neither app transposes standard notation from MusicXML.
- Both apps handle face-gesture turns, but MobileSheets includes them in the one-time price while forScore requires Pro.

### Gaps
- MobileSheets external display and HDMI behaviour on iPad: no explicit vendor doc found (dual-tablet pairing exists).
- Whether MobileSheets on iOS has full parity with the Android version: no definitive source.
- YouTube links attached to songs: not confirmed for either app from the sources fetched.
- forScore "Reflow": what it does with MusicXML was not documented in the pages I fetched.

## MobileSheets vs forScore: strengths, weaknesses, complaints

### Takeaway
User reports (mostly 2023–2025, heavily from the MobileSheets forum) favor MobileSheets for half-page turns, metadata and library flexibility, formats, cross-platform use, price and fast developer support. forScore is praised for polish, Pencil integration and iCloud sync, and as the established standard. Neither ships built-in fake-book indexes. Several users switched from forScore to MobileSheets; switching the other way is rarely reported. That proves little, given where the reports were found.

### Cited Findings
- MobileSheets developer support: emails answered within a day and bugs often fixed within days, "this sold me". A "Swap File" feature replaces a PDF while keeping its collection and setlist memberships. Built-in backup/restore and peer-to-peer sync. Editable, sortable metadata. "MobileSheets is awesome" after a year of daily use (May 2024) [user]. — [TidBITS](https://talk.tidbits.com/t/choosing-a-sheet-music-manager-for-ipad/21920)
- MobileSheets cons in the same thread: no built-in song indexes, no native iCloud or Dropbox library sync at the time (2023), and occasional crashes that were usually fixed [user]. A 2026 update notes "iCloud library sync" fixes, so iCloud sync now exists [store listing]. — [TidBITS](https://talk.tidbits.com/t/choosing-a-sheet-music-manager-for-ipad/21920); [App Store MobileSheets](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1620569987?l=en-us)
- forScore in the same thread: "extremely capable", good PDF editing, custom metadata and setlists, iCloud across Mac, iPad and iPhone, "handles many jazz fake books without problems". Cons: support took a week to reply, there are no built-in indexes, and one user called it "basically a souped-up PDF reader" with no spreadsheet-style song data [user, 2023]. — [TidBITS](https://talk.tidbits.com/t/choosing-a-sheet-music-manager-for-ipad/21920)
- Half-page turns: one user called forScore's half-page turn a "disaster" that forces the eyes to jump to the top, and preferred MobileSheets' approach. Another user found forScore's "works flawlessly". MobileSheets also lets you link audio such as Music Minus One tracks and keep separate "autonomous libraries", which forScore lacks [user, May–Jul 2024]. — [Zubersoft forum thread 11441](https://www.zubersoft.com/mobilesheets/forum/thread-11441.html)
- Switchers: one user found forScore hard to learn and MobileSheets intuitive in minutes, and dropped OnSong and forScore within 3 months. App Store reviews include "Divorced ForScore after meeting MobileSheets" [user]. The opposite view: a forScore user since v1.0 found MobileSheets "unintuitive, restrictive and a bit ugly" by comparison [user]. One reviewer noted that "the really slick features cost extra in forScore" [user]. — [appshunter MobileSheets reviews](https://appshunter.io/ios/app/1620569987/reviews/2); [Zubersoft forum post 34248](https://zubersoft.com/mobilesheets/forum/post-34248.html)
- An independent reviewer calls forScore "the gold standard" and the best iPad viewer [3rd-party]. — [Medium: Best Sheet Music Reader for iPad](https://medium.com/the-modern-musician/best-sheet-music-reader-for-ipad-bbbf328ced84)

### Inferences
- For this user (already on MobileSheets, iPad, PDFs of the 557 and other fake books, plus play-along audio), I see no feature gap that forScore would clearly fill. Its main advantages are polish, the larger iOS community, Watch/Cue integration and documented external display. Switching would cost $24.99, plus $14.99/yr for face gestures, plus re-indexing the library.
- MobileSheets' ChordPro support and Android/Windows availability keep a later device change open.

### Gaps
- No independent, recent (2025–26) head-to-head review such as Scoring Notes was found. Scoring Notes has a multi-app iPad comparison, but its date and conclusions were not retrieved.
- MobileSheets iOS performance with very large (500+ page) PDFs vs forScore: no data found.

## Fake-book index files (CSV) for these apps

### Takeaway
Free community CSV indexes exist for most Real and fake books. The main collection is the GitHub repo aspiers/book-indices, which forScore imports directly. The MobileSheets forum hosts one thread per book with user CSVs, and MobileSheets uses CSVs to split a songbook PDF into songs. Expect page-offset and edition mismatches.

### Cited Findings
- **aspiers/book-indices** (GitHub, 61 stars, 44 forks, 171 commits) is a collaboratively edited set of CSVs mapping titles to pages. It covers Real Book 1–6, including the 6th edition and Bb/Bass versions, New Real Book 1–3, Real Christmas, Real R&B, Vocal Real Book, Brazilian Jazz Real Book, Jazz Fake, JazzLTD, Ultimate Pop Rock Fake Book, Sher Standards, Colorado Book, Evans Book, Great Gig Book, New Orleans Jambook, The Slick Book, Pat Metheny and others. The README names forScore as importing them, plus PDF splitter tools (fakebook-splitter, PDFexploder, splitPDF, Birdland Musician's Assistant) [3rd-party]. — [GitHub aspiers/book-indices](https://github.com/aspiers/book-indices)
- forScore maps CSV columns itself, so field order doesn't matter, and you may need a page offset (for example Real Book 1 v6 needs −9) [user]. forScore also has a built-in "Index" button in bookmarks that guesses titles and pages from a PDF's table of contents; a user found it worked well but needed fixes (2019, older info). — [robbyburns.com (2019)](https://robbyburns.com/blog/uac5awql1zh7vdrabhbdhmgxhmjl6h); [GitHub ndp/musicindex](https://github.com/ndp/musicindex)
- MobileSheets: the forum has one thread per book with user CSVs. For import, the CSV filename must match the PDF filename. A CSV can be built from scratch with just Song Name and Page # columns [user]. — [Zubersoft forum thread 8473](https://www.zubersoft.com/mobilesheets/forum/thread-8473.html); [Zubersoft forum tid=3197](https://www.zubersoft.com/mobilesheets/forum/showthread.php?tid=3197); [Ultimate Pop Rock Fake Book thread](https://www.zubersoft.com/mobilesheets/forum/showthread.php?tid=8474)
- Pitfall: an iGigBook App Store reviewer found a digital edition linked to the index of a print edition with different songs. Offsets of a few pages are common [user]. — [App Store iGigBook](https://apps.apple.com/app/id396323113)
- The TidBITS thread points to free CSV collections and the MobileSheets forum as good sources for forScore users too [user]. — [TidBITS](https://talk.tidbits.com/t/choosing-a-sheet-music-manager-for-ipad/21920)

### Inferences
- The same CSVs work in both apps, possibly after column remapping, so indexes don't tie the user to either app.
- For the 557 Standards (the band's book), check the MobileSheets forum and aspiers/book-indices for an existing CSV. Neither result listed one explicitly, and building one by hand (title + page) is simple.

### Gaps
- Whether a CSV index for "557 Jazz Standards" exists was not confirmed.
- The last-update date of aspiers/book-indices was not visible.
