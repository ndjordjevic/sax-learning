# Autumn Leaves (557 p.21): 🔄 learning

**Done (2026-11-18):** ✅ listening · melody in the head · melody and guide tones from the sheet · following the changes by ear over Playbook backing track 1.

**Next (2026-11-21):**
1. Warm up on the chord tones over a drone (concert G drone = tenor A for the minor half).
2. Over a backing track, very slowly, add one layer at a time, following the chart:
   1. roots;
   2. root + 5th (*Let's Play Jazz!* Ch.3 p.12);
   3. guide tones (Playbook);
   4. arpeggios.
3. Stick to the Playbook and *Let's Play Jazz!* for now.
4. This is also the first tune for step 5 of the daily plan (start improvising).

**Material**
- **Playbook** study #1. The key is concert G minor = tenor A minor, the same as the band.
- Tenor lead sheet from the 557 (`6 Fake Books/`). It uses the 557 harmony, not the Playbook's.
- Our Autumn Leaves chord chart (tenor chords, guide tones, guitar shapes; PDF in the Playbook folder).
- **Listening:**
  - Cannonball/Miles, *Somethin' Else*. This is the companion recording, Cannonball's phrasing.
  - Bill Evans, *Portrait in Jazz*.
  - Ben Webster.
- **Tenor chords:** Dm7 G7 Cmaj7 Fmaj7 · Bm7♭5 E7 Am6.
- **iReal Pro** shows Am6 where the 557 has Am7; edit the chart in the app if you want it to match.

**Extras only this tune has** (*Let's Play Jazz!*, for steps 5–7 of [how to learn a standard](../band/how-to-learn-a-standard.md)):
- root + 5th, then arpeggios → scales → digit patterns over Play-Along 2 (Ch.3);
- *Falling Leaves*: a new melody on the same changes (tenor chart p.9), copied from Listening Track 1;
- easy solo chorus pp.30–31 (Track 4); the advanced choruses (Tracks 5–6) come later;
- improvise over Play-Along 3;
- once Birch comes off hold (after Ch.1–7): A minor pentatonic (A C D E G on tenor) over Play-Along 2. It fits every chord but doesn't "make the changes".
