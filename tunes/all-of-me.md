# All of Me (Marks & Simons; 557 p.8): ⏭ after *All Blues*

Material collected 2026-11-24. Not started yet.

**Key:** concert C major = tenor D major (Playbook Bb edition). 32 bars; the bandmate's list has no tempo. Chords on tenor: D6 · F♯7 · B7 · Em7 · A7 · Bm7 · E7 · … · back to D6, with a quick jump to the key of D minor (Gm6) near the end.

Files: the fake books are in `6 Fake Books/`; the tune-specific files are in `6 Fake Books/Band Tunes/All of Me/`.
